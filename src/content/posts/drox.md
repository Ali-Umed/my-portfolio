**Drox (درۆکس)** puts every loyalty card in one app. Customers collect stamps with a single QR code and unlock real rewards, while businesses run their loyalty programs, offers, team and live stats from one dashboard. No plastic cards, no paper stamps.

I built the whole product: the **iOS and Android app**, the **business dashboard** and the **drox.cards website**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎟️ Loyalty Cards with QR Stamps
- **One QR for everything**: the business scans the customer's personal QR with the staff app, the phone buzzes and the stamp appears live.
- **Progress at a glance**: every card shows its stamps and the next reward, with "Almost there" when one stamp is left.
- **Renewable or one-time**: renewable cards start a fresh round automatically; one-time cards lock after the reward is redeemed.

### 🔎 Find Cards and Offers
- **Categories and featured cards**: browse sixteen categories, from coffee to car wash, or swipe the Featured carousel.
- **Time-limited offers**: deals and gifts from businesses nearby, with live countdowns before they run out.
- **Map**: every branch that runs a Drox card, filtered by category.
- **Trending**: the most popular cards, refreshed every seven days by how many new customers stamped in.

### 📊 Business Dashboard
- **Card designer**: multi-step loyalty cards with a category, background template or own colour, and the number of stamps for each reward.
- **Offers**: discount products with start and end dates, daily time windows and countdowns.
- **Staff accounts**: team members get scoped permissions (Offers, Loyalty or all) and scan customer QR codes from their own phone.
- **Live analytics**: claims, redemptions, redemption rate per card, stamp velocity and a top-10 customer leaderboard.

### ⚡ Easy for Everyone
- **One-tap sign-in**: Google or Apple, no phone number and no OTP codes.
- **Three languages**: Kurdish Sorani, Arabic and English, with right-to-left layouts.
- **Dark design**: a dark interface with warm orange accents.
