**nexa-iq.com** is the official website of the **Nexa Edu** education app. It introduces the app to students and schools, shows its real screens and sends visitors to the App Store or Google Play.

Nexa was built **as a team**; on this website I was the **web developer**, building it with **Next.js 16** and **React 19**. On the [Nexa Edu app](/posts/nexa-edu/) I worked as the Flutter developer.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Landing page**: hero with store buttons, a showcase of the app's screens and its main features.
- **About and contact pages**: Nexa's mission and vision, with email, phone and office details.
- **Download page and QR code**: one place to get the app on iPhone or Android.
- **Legal pages**: privacy policy, terms and conditions, and account deletion, as required by the stores.
- **Three languages**: Kurdish Sorani, Arabic and English with right-to-left layouts, using next-intl.
- **Light and dark themes** with a scroll progress bar and smooth Framer Motion animations, on every screen size.
