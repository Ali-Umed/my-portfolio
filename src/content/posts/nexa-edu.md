**Nexa Edu (نێکسا)** is an education platform that brings school courses, practice and teachers together in one mobile app. Students follow lessons by stage and semester, test themselves with timed quizzes, ask their teacher directly and buy new courses inside the app.

I built the whole product: the **iOS and Android app** and the **Nexa website** with its legal and account pages.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📚 Courses by Stage
- **Organised lessons**: every course is split into semesters, chapters and short lessons, like *Algebra Fundamentals → Linear Equations → Solving Two-Step Equations*.
- **Teacher profiles**: each course shows its teacher, the number of lessons and the school stage.
- **Ask Teacher**: students can send a question to the course teacher from any lesson.

### ✏️ Practice & Timed Quizzes
- **Practice setup**: pick the chapters to practise and how many questions to answer.
- **Timed quizzes**: a countdown keeps practice focused; multiple-choice answers are checked instantly.
- **Clear rules**: students see the rules before they start, such as no pausing once a quiz begins.

### 💳 In-App Course Purchases
- **Local payment methods**: courses can be bought with **FIB (First Iraqi Bank)** or **FastPay** wallets.
- **Clear pricing**: price in IQD and how long access lasts are shown before paying.
- **Secure checkout**: payments are processed securely, and access opens straight away.

### 🌐 Built for the Region
- **Three languages**: Kurdish Sorani, Arabic and English, with right-to-left layouts.
- **Dark design**: a calm dark interface with orange and purple accents, easy on the eyes for long study sessions.
- **Privacy first**: students can see what data is kept and request account deletion at any time.
