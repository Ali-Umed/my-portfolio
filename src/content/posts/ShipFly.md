# 📦 Ocean Road

**Ocean Road** is a smart, modern Flutter application designed for **tracking packages** across **air (fly)**, **sea (container/ship)**, and **land (vehicle/truck)**.

Track your orders **live** with real-time updates on where your package is:  

- 🛫 In the air  
- 🚢 In the container  
- 🚛 On the road  
- 📍 Or already in your city!

> **🔍 Know exactly what’s inside, where it is, and when it will arrive — all in one place!**

## 🚀 Features

- 🌍 **Multi-Method Tracking**
  - Track packages by **air**, **sea**, or **land**.
  - Know the **current location**, **status**, and **destination**.

- 🧾 **Package Details**
  - See what's inside the package with item summaries.

- 🌐 **Multi-Language Support**
  - Available in **Kurdish**, **Arabic**, and **English**.

- 🌙 **Dark & Light Theme**
  - Smooth switch between light and dark modes for comfort.

- 👤 **User Profile**
  - View your tracking history and manage your preferences.

- 🗺 **Interactive Map View**
  - Visualize your package moving through the world in real time.

- 🔔 **Notifications**
  - Get alerts when your package moves to a new stage.

- 📱 **Cross-Platform**
  - Runs on Android and iOS with seamless experience.
