**drox.cards** is the official website of the **Drox** digital loyalty cards app. It explains how Drox works for shoppers and businesses, shows the app's real screens, and sends visitors to the right store to download it.

I designed and built the whole site with **Next.js 16** and **React 19**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Landing page**: hero, real app screens, a four-step "how it works" walkthrough, industries, trending cards and FAQ.
- **Business page**: everything a shop needs to know about running loyalty cards, offers, staff scanners and live analytics on Drox.
- **Card pages**: every loyalty card has its own shareable page at `drox.cards/card/…`.
- **Downloads page**: App Store and Google Play links for iPhone and Android.
- **Three languages**: Kurdish Sorani, Arabic and English with right-to-left layouts, using next-intl.
- **SEO**: sitemap, robots, Open Graph images and search-friendly content for every language.
- **Legal pages**: privacy policy and account deletion, as required by the stores.
- **Light and dark themes** with smooth Framer Motion animations, on every screen size.
