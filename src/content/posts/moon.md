# 🕌 مانگ – Islamic Q&A App

**مانگ** is a modern Flutter application focused on Islamic education through quizzes and answers.  
It features authentic questions from the **Qur’an**, **Hadith**, **Fiqh**, and general Islamic knowledge.  
Built with **Flutter**, integrated with **Firebase** for real-time features and user management.

## ✨ Features

- 📖 **Islamic Questions** – Curated from Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, and daily Islamic life
- 🔢 **Challenge Mode** – 100-question challenge that ends when you get one wrong
- 🎯 **Score System** – Track and improve your performance
- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications** – Stay informed with Firebase push notifications
- 🎨 **Light & Dark Mode** – Switch between themes with beautiful modern colors
- ❓ **5+ Question Types** – Multiple choice, true/false, image-based, verse matching, and more
- 🧠 **Educational Experience** – Designed for learners of all ages
